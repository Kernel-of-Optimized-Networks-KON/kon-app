# Sample image attribution

`kon-sample.dng` is the demo photograph behind **Try sample** (Compress) and **Open sample** (PKON Viewer).
It is bundled byte-for-byte unmodified (original camera DNG, all metadata intact).

- **Camera / format:** RICOH GR III, camera-native DNG 1.2, single-plane Bayer (RGGB) CFA, 14-bit, lossless-JPEG compressed, stored raster 6112x4064 (active area 6000x4000)
- **Subject:** coastal dunes with marram grass, beach, sea and clouds
